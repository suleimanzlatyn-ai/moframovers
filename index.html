Who Mofra Movers is

Mofra Movers is a Nairobi-based moving and storage company. Public business profiles describe the company as serving residential and commercial customers with moving, relocation, packing, storage and transportation services, including selected routes across East Africa.

  • Home, apartment and office relocation
  • Packing and careful transport support
  • Storage solutions
  • Transportation within Kenya
  • Selected cross-border and overseas moving support

Mission & vision

The company's public profile emphasizes respect and dedication in its staff, practical solutions for client needs, reliability, safety and timely service.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you move houses and apartments?

Yes. House and apartment moving is listed among Mofra Movers' services.

Do you relocate offices?

Yes. Office relocation is listed as a Mofra Movers service.

Do you offer packing?

Yes. Packing is listed among the company's moving services.

Do you provide storage?

Yes. Storage is listed as a service for customers who need temporary holding.

How much does a move cost?

Pricing depends on locations, volume, access, labour, packing and transportation requirements. Request a quote for your move.

Do you move outside Nairobi?

Public business listings describe Mofra Movers as operating across Kenya and on selected East African routes.

Which details should I provide for a quote?

Give the pickup and destination, move type, approximate size or inventory, preferred date, access or floor restrictions, and whether you need packing or storage.

Do you operate in Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda?

Public company information identifies these countries among Mofra Movers' East African routes. Confirm availability and route-specific requirements before booking.

Do you offer overseas moving?

Public business information lists moving overseas among the company's services. Contact Mofra for the details of your destination and shipment requirements.

Do you provide a fixed online price?

The website does not publish a universal price because moving costs depend on the move. Request a quote with your locations, load and requirements.
