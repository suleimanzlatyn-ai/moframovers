Mofra Movers — Moving Company in Kenya

Mofra Movers is a Nairobi-based moving company providing house removals, office relocation, packing, storage and transportation services across Kenya and into East Africa.

Moving services across Kenya

Mofra Movers serves customers who need to move homes, offices and belongings within Nairobi and between locations in Kenya. The company also provides cross-border transportation and moving services in East Africa.

House and office relocation

Services include house removals and office relocation, with packing and transportation available as part of the moving process.

Storage services

Mofra Movers provides storage for customers who need temporary space for household or office items during a relocation.

East Africa moving

The company lists service coverage across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi, making it possible to arrange cross-border moving from its Nairobi base.
