Office Relocation in Nairobi

Mofra Movers helps businesses relocate office furniture, equipment, files and other business contents in Nairobi and across Kenya. Office moving can include planning, packing and handling, transportation and delivery support.

Business moving support

Before an office move, identify the furniture, equipment and files that need to travel, the pickup and destination addresses, access restrictions and the preferred moving date. This information helps the moving team plan the work.

Office moving checklist

  • Create an inventory of furniture and equipment.
  • Separate sensitive documents and valuables.
  • Confirm parking, loading access, lifts and stairs.
  • Plan where desks and equipment should be placed at the destination.
  • Confirm whether packing or storage is required.
  • Choose a moving window that limits disruption to staff and customers.
