Moving Checklist for Homes and Offices in Kenya

Use this checklist before moving day to reduce surprises and make the relocation easier to organise.

Before moving day

□ Confirm the pickup and delivery addresses.
□ Confirm the moving date and preferred time.
□ Make an inventory of large furniture and appliances.
□ Separate valuables, documents and personal essentials.
□ Identify fragile, heavy or oversized items.
□ Confirm lift, stairs, parking and loading access.
□ Decide whether you need packing support.
□ Decide whether you need storage between locations.

For an office move

□ Label desks, equipment and files by destination area.
□ Plan downtime so staff know when the move will happen.
□ Back up important digital information.
□ Keep sensitive documents under appropriate control.

On moving day

□ Keep the agreed access route clear.
□ Keep essentials separate from items going on the moving truck.
□ Check the pickup inventory before departure.
□ Confirm the destination contact person.

After delivery

□ Check that the agreed items arrived.
□ Direct furniture and boxes to their intended rooms or areas.
□ Report any issue promptly to the moving company.
