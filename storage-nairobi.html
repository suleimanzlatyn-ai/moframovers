Storage in Nairobi

Mofra Movers provides storage support for customers who need a place for belongings during a move, property transition, renovation or office relocation. Storage can be discussed together with moving and transportation requirements.

When storage can help

  • Your new home is not ready on the same day as your old one.
  • You are renovating and need furniture out of the way.
  • An office is changing premises and needs temporary space.
  • You are between properties and need time before final delivery.

Combine storage with moving

Tell the team what needs to be moved, where it is located, the expected storage period and where the items will eventually be delivered. This makes it easier to discuss the full moving plan.
