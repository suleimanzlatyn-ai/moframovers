About Mofra Movers

Mofra Movers is a Nairobi-based moving company serving homes and businesses with relocation, transportation, packing and storage support. Public business listings identify the company with George Padmore Lane, off Ngong Road, opposite Uchumi Hyper, Dove Centre, Nairobi.

Our services

  • House and apartment moving
  • Office relocation
  • Packing and handling
  • Moving transportation
  • Storage support
  • Selected cross-border moving and transportation

Where we operate

Mofra Movers is based in Nairobi and serves customers across Kenya. The company also handles selected East African routes involving countries such as Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda.
