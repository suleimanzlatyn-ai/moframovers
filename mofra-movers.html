About us

A decade of moving Kenya.

Mofra Movers Ltd has been relocating homes and offices across Kenya and East Africa since 2014, based on Ngong Road in Nairobi.

We started as a local Nairobi moving crew and have grown into a full-service operation covering household removals, office relocation, secure storage, parcel and single-item delivery, and cross-border moves into Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi — including customs clearance and border documentation.

Every move comes with a personal, customer-first approach: a clear upfront assessment, honest communication, and a crew that treats your belongings like their own. We even help reconnect DSTV, Zuku and Startimes at your new home.
