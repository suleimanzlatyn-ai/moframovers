Moving Costs in Nairobi: What Affects Your Quote?

There is no single price for every move in Nairobi. The cost depends on the distance, amount of property, access at both locations, packing requirements, timing and whether storage or special handling is needed.

1. Size of the move

A bedsitter, one-bedroom apartment and large family home require different amounts of labour, vehicle space and time. Give the mover a realistic description of furniture, appliances and boxes.

2. Distance and route

The pickup and destination affect transport requirements. Moving within Nairobi can be different from an inter-county or cross-border move.

3. Access and building conditions

Stairs, lifts, narrow entrances, parking restrictions and long carrying distances can change the work involved. Mention these details before receiving a quote.

4. Packing and fragile items

If you need packing or handling help, say so. Fragile, heavy or unusually large items may require additional planning.

5. Storage

If your new property is not ready, you may need storage between pickup and final delivery. Discuss the expected storage period with the moving company.

How to request a useful quote

  1. Give both addresses or areas.
  2. Give the moving date.
  3. Describe the property size.
  4. List large furniture and appliances.
  5. Mention stairs, lifts and parking.
  6. Say whether packing or storage is required.
