Mofra Movers Nairobi

Mofra Movers is a Nairobi-based moving company serving homes and businesses in Kenya and across East Africa. Our services include house removals, office relocation, packing, storage and transportation.

Moving services in Nairobi

If you are moving within Nairobi or relocating to another part of Kenya, Mofra Movers provides a single point of contact for planning, packing, transportation and delivery. We also handle moves that cross national borders in East Africa.

House moving and home removals

Moving house can involve furniture, appliances, boxes and personal belongings. Mofra Movers provides house removal and transportation services for customers moving within Nairobi and between Kenyan towns and cities.

Office relocation in Nairobi

For businesses changing offices, Mofra Movers provides office removal and relocation services. The service is designed for moving office furniture, equipment and packed items from one location to another.

Packing and storage

Mofra Movers also provides packing and storage services. These services can be useful when a move requires temporary storage before delivery or when household and office items need to be packed before transportation.

Where Mofra Movers operates

The company is based on Ngong Road in Nairobi and serves customers in Kenya, with cross-border services extending to Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi.
