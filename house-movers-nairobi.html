House Movers in Nairobi

Mofra Movers provides household relocation support for apartments, bedsitters, family homes and other residential moves in Nairobi. The service can include moving, packing and handling, transportation and storage support.

Apartment and house moving

Whether you are moving between Nairobi neighbourhoods or relocating to another part of Kenya, start by sharing your pickup address, destination, moving date and the approximate size of the move. This helps the team understand access and transport requirements.

Packing and handling

Household items can include furniture, appliances, boxes, kitchen items and fragile belongings. Tell Mofra Movers about fragile or unusually large items so the moving plan can account for them.

Moving checklist

  • Confirm the pickup and delivery addresses.
  • Reserve lifts or loading access where required.
  • Separate documents, valuables and personal essentials.
  • Identify fragile, heavy or oversized items.
  • Confirm the preferred moving date and timing.
  • Ask about packing or storage if you need either service.
